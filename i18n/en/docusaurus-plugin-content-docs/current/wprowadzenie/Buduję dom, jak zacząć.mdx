## Planning a smart home installation

A well-planned installation means that the more elements you include at the design stage, the easier and cheaper it will be to expand your smart home system later.

### For planning you can use:
- Excel
- LibreOffice Draw
- classic building floor plans

## Project breakdown:

When planning, it is worth going through each of the points below:

- AC lighting circuits
- LED lighting circuits
- Controlled circuits / sockets
- Roller shutters / blinds / awnings
- Gates / wickets / doors
- Heating / cooling / HVAC / heat recovery / temperature and air quality sensors
- Control panels / tablets
- Weather station
- Garden irrigation
- Multimedia / audio / smart speakers with voice analysis
- Alarm / sensors: motion, presence, fire, flooding / water shut-off valves
- LAN / Wi-Fi / fibre connection
- Cameras / monitoring
- Technical automation / pumps / booster pumps / energy control
- Photovoltaics / energy storage
- Electric car charger
- Distribution boards / RACK cabinets

:::tip
At the very beginning, estimate the number of elements in each group, e.g. 25 AC lights, 6 shutters, 3 gates, 5 heating zones + sensors, 2 smart sockets, motion alarm in every room + flood sensors in the kitchen and bathroom, PV + charging station.
:::

### AC lighting circuits

Control of classic 230V AC lighting.

**Functions:**
- 230V circuits on / off
- Light scenes
- Schedules
- Integration with motion, presence and twilight sensors
- Presence simulation

---

### LED lighting circuits

Control of LED lighting: WHITE, CCT, RGB, RGBW

**Functions:**
- PWM dimming
- Light scenes, e.g. depending on the time of day, behaviour or special ones like party
- Integration with automation
- Presence simulation

---

### Controlled circuits / sockets

Control of sockets and loads.

**Functions:**
- Remote control
- Schedules
- Energy metering (optional)

---

### Roller shutters / blinds / awnings

Control of window coverings.

**Functions:**
- Up / down / stop
- Percentage positioning
- Slat angle positioning for blinds
- Weather automation
- Overheating protection
- Noise protection
- Burglary protection

---

### Gates / wickets / doors

Building access control.

**Functions:**
- Open / close
- Access control
- Remote opening for a courier

---

### HVAC / heating / cooling / heat recovery / sensors

Building climate control.

**Functions:**
- Building heating
- Temperature sensors
- Domestic hot water heating, tank heating on demand
- Room ventilation, e.g. bathroom
- Zone heating
- Air conditioning
- Heat recovery ventilation
- CO2 / humidity monitoring

---

### Control panels / tablets

System user interface.

**Functions:**
- Control of the whole building
- Room views
- Scenes
- Shortcuts

---

### Weather station

External measuring system.

**Parameters:**
- Temperature
- Humidity
- Wind
- Sunlight
- Precipitation
- Shutter automation
- HVAC
- Weather logic

---

### Garden irrigation

Automatic irrigation systems.

**Functions:**
- Valve control
- Schedules
- Soil moisture sensors

---

### Multimedia / audio / smart speakers

Audio and voice communication system.

**Functions:**
- Multi-room audio
- Voice control
- System announcements
- Alarm announcements

---

### Alarm / sensors / security

Building security system.

**Sensors:**
- Motion
- Presence
- Smoke / fire
- Flooding
- Alarm
- Notifications
- Water shut-off
- Silent alarm call function

---

### LAN / Wi-Fi / fibre

Communication infrastructure of the system.

**Elements:**
- Routers
- Switches
- Access points
- Fibre optics

---

### Cameras / monitoring

Video system.

**Functions:**
- Video recording
- Live view
- Motion detection
- Alarm integration

---

### Technical automation

Control of technical equipment.

**Devices:**
- Pumps
- Booster pumps
- Valves
- Motors

---

### Photovoltaics / energy storage

Energy management.

**Functions:**
- PV monitoring
- Battery management
- Energy balance
- Consumption optimisation

---

### Electric car charger

EV control.

**Functions:**
- Charging schedule
- Dynamic load management
- PV integration

---

### Distribution boards / RACK cabinets

Central installation infrastructure.

**Elements:**
- Electrical distribution boards
- RACK cabinets
- Controllers
- Emergency power
