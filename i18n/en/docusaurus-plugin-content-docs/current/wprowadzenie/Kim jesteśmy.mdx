## SMARTBOB history

SMARTBOB AUTOMATYKA SP. Z O.O. was founded on 4 December 2023 by Krzysztof Bogus and Michał Góral, who combined engineering experience with hands-on practice in building automation and smart home integration.

The company grew out of the need for an open and flexible controller platform that does not tie the user to one ecosystem or one manufacturer. The idea came up while working on real automation installations, where available solutions were often closed, inflexible or hard to integrate.

At first the project was developed as an experimental and installation solution, but it quickly turned into a full line of home automation controllers.

Today SMARTBOB is a growing controller platform combining electronics, software and system integration into one consistent solution for smart buildings.

## Our controllers

We design and manufacture building automation controllers for TH35 rails in Poland – from a small gate module to central units for the whole house. All of them run on an ESP32 processor, have plug-in connectors, LAN and WiFi and work with SUPLA, Home Assistant (ESPHome), Loxone, Ampio and MQTT. You can explore every model in 3D in the [3D technical assistant](https://smartbobautomatyka.github.io/SmartBob3D/#lang=en).

## Achievements

- SMARTBOB has sold over 1500 controllers since the company was founded in 12.2023 and built a base of over 300 unique customers (as of 05.2026). The company keeps expanding its product line with new models and solutions.
- SMARTBOB is an official partner of the SUPLA platform and integrates with popular smart home systems. It received an EU grant under the PCINN Akceleracja programme for producing the controller in Poland worth PLN 220,000. The company took part in development and acceleration programmes such as Garage Genius, Innovation Coach, Pitch Me Baby and Startup Wrocław, and cooperates with the Wrocław University of Science and Technology (KMEiF department, GoSmart student club).
- SMARTBOB attends industry events, including PTAK Smart Home Expo, Poznań Smart Home, Made in Wrocław, Best Career Wrocław and EMC for Business.
