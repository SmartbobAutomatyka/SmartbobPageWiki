## Controller SM-LITE-1624D

SM-LITE-1624D is the controller for LED lighting and DC loads: 24 transistor outputs with smooth PWM dimming (5–50 V DC) and 16 inputs for push buttons and sensors – LED strips, fans, solenoid valves and DC drives in one module.

## Key features

- 16 control inputs 24 V (GND or VCC signal) – push buttons, switches, sensors
- 24 independent N-MOSFET outputs 5–50 V DC with PWM – LEDs, fans, motors, solenoid valves
- 2 ADC channels up to 24 V
- LAN or Wi-Fi communication
- RS485, I2C and 1-Wire
- 0.96" OLED display
- 24 V DC supply

Designed for a distribution board – central control of DC-based installations: from LED lighting through ventilation to drives and solenoid valves.

---
## Applications

- Automation of houses and flats with LED lighting
- Many LED circuits and DC drives from one controller
- Dimming and light scenes (PWM)
- Alarm integration via sensors
- Scenes and schedules
- Solenoid valves, fans and other DC loads
- Smart home integration (SUPLA, Home Assistant, LOXONE, AMPIO, HOMEY)

## Peripherals

1. **Transistor outputs (MOSFET)** – N-MOSFET outputs 5–50 V DC with PWM – LED strips, fans, solenoid valves; load minus to OUT.
2. **Output GND terminals** – Common minus of the output group – ground of the DC load supply.
3. **Control inputs** – 24 V digital inputs – push buttons, switches, sensors.
4. **Power connector (24V DC)** – Main power supply of the controller.
5. **ADC channels** – Analog inputs – voltage measurement and analog sensors (e.g. 0–10 V).
6. **RS485 interface** – Modbus RS485 communication with external devices.
7. **Ethernet port (RJ45)** – LAN connection.
8. **Universal inputs and expansion interfaces** – I2C, 1-Wire and other bus connectors.
9. **USB-C connector** – Programming, configuration and service.
10. **Configuration button** – Service button for configuration and reset.
11. **OLED display** – Status display, visible through the housing.
12. **LED indicators** – Input, output and operation status LEDs – visible through the housing.

## Safe use

Before installation make sure the wires are not live. Installation should only be carried out by a qualified person in accordance with applicable standards. Do not use the controller without a housing or with a damaged housing. Do not exceed the operating parameters – especially the relay load. Some platforms require an external server, cloud or additional software.
