## Controller SM-LITE-1616R

SM-LITE-1616R is the heart of a smart home in the distribution board: 16 inputs for push buttons and sensors and 16 relays of 10 A that switch the lights, lower the shutters, open the gate and start the heating – all from one DIN-rail module.

## Key features

- 16 logic inputs 24 V (GND or VCC signal)
- 2 ADC channels up to 24 V
- 16 independent 10 A relays, potential-free contacts
- LAN or Wi-Fi communication
- Two I2C buses (external and internal)
- RS485, 1-Wire and Bluetooth
- 0.96" OLED display
- Two TMP102 temperature sensors – thermal protection (up to 80 °C, set in YAML)
- 24 V DC supply (max 0.5 A)
- Service features: USB programmer, input test buttons, reset

The controller is designed for installation in an electrical distribution board.

## Applications

- Whole-house automation
- Control of many lighting circuits and shutters
- Alarm integration via sensors
- Scenes and schedules
- HVAC via RS485 / Modbus
- Smart home integration (SUPLA, Home Assistant, MQTT)
- Energy and parameter monitoring

## Power supply parameters

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Rated supply voltage | 24V DC |
| Supply voltage range | 22V – 26V DC |
| Current consumption (max.) | 0,5 A (12 W) |
| Protection | Polymer fuse (Polyfuse) |

:::tip
Several SMARTBOB controllers in one installation can share one power supply with sufficient current capacity, but a separate power supply is recommended.
:::

:::tip
When powered only from USB, the inputs and outputs do not work – the controller starts and connects to the network.
:::

## Safe use

Before installation make sure the wires are not live. Installation should only be carried out by a qualified person in accordance with applicable standards. Do not use the controller without a housing or with a damaged housing. Do not exceed the operating parameters – especially the relay load. Some platforms require an external server, cloud or additional software.
