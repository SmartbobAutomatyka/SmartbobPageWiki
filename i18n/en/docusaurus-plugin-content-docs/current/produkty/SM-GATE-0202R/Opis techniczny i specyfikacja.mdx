## Controller SM-GATE-0202R

SM-GATE-0202R is a small controller for gates and wickets: 2 relays of 10 A, 2 fully isolated inputs and an optional MOBILUS 868 MHz remote receiver – in 4 DIN modules.

## Key features

- 2 fully isolated AC/DC inputs (optocouplers) – e.g. gate drive signal, limit switches, photocells
- 2 ADC channels up to 24 V
- 2 independent 10 A relays, potential-free contacts – e.g. the gate opening signal
- LAN or Wi-Fi communication
- 0.96" OLED display
- Optional MOBILUS 868 MHz radio module – gate remotes
- 24 V AC/DC supply (any polarity)

For a distribution board or a box by the gate – controls a gate, wicket or other circuit on its own or as an extension of the installation.

---
## Applications

- Entrance gates, wickets and garage doors
- MOBILUS 868 MHz remotes (radio module version)
- Small standalone control points in a larger installation
- Limit switches, reed contacts and photocells in alarm and automation
- Scenes and schedules
- Smart home integration (SUPLA, Home Assistant, LOXONE)

## Peripherals

1. **Relay outputs** – Relay outputs for controlling lighting, shutters, devices and AC/DC circuits.
2. **AC/DC inputs (isolated)** – Two isolated inputs – AC or DC signal from the gate drive, limit switches or photocells.
3. **Power connector (24 V AC/DC)** – 24 V AC or DC supply – any polarity (bridge rectifier).
4. **ADC channels** – Analog inputs – voltage measurement and analog sensors (e.g. 0–10 V).
5. **RS485 interface** – Modbus RS485 communication with external devices.
6. **Ethernet port (RJ45)** – LAN connection.
7. **Universal inputs and expansion interfaces** – I2C, 1-Wire and other bus connectors.
8. **USB-C connector** – Programming, configuration and service.
9. **MOBILUS 868 MHz radio module** – Optional receiver for MOBILUS gate remotes.
10. **OLED display** – Status display, visible through the housing.
11. **LED indicators** – Input, output and operation status LEDs – visible through the housing.

## Safe use

Before installation make sure the wires are not live. Installation should only be carried out by a qualified person in accordance with applicable standards. Do not use the controller without a housing or with a damaged housing. Do not exceed the operating parameters – especially the relay load. Some platforms require an external server, cloud or additional software.
