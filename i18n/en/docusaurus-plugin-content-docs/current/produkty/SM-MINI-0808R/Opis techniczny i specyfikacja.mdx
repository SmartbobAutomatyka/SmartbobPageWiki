## Controller SM-MINI-0808R

SM-MINI-0808R is a compact controller in 7 DIN modules: 8 inputs and 8 relays of 10 A – ideal for a flat, a garage or as an extension of a larger SMARTBOB installation.

## Key features

- 8 control inputs 24 V (GND or VCC signal) – push buttons, switches, sensors
- 2 ADC channels up to 24 V
- 8 independent 10 A relays, potential-free contacts
- LAN or Wi-Fi communication
- Two I2C buses (external and internal) and 1-Wire
- 0.96" OLED display
- TMP102 temperature sensor – thermal protection
- 24 V DC supply

Designed for a distribution board – control of a smaller installation: from lighting and shutters through heating to gates.

---
## Applications

- Smaller flats, houses and single rooms
- Lighting, shutters and loads from one compact controller
- Alarm integration via sensors
- Scenes and schedules
- Extending a larger SMARTBOB installation with extra control points
- Smart home integration (SUPLA, Home Assistant, LOXONE, AMPIO, HOMEY)

1. **Relay outputs** – Relay outputs for controlling lighting, shutters, devices and AC/DC circuits.
2. **Control inputs** – 24 V digital inputs – push buttons, switches, sensors.
3. **Power connector (24V DC)** – Main power supply of the controller.
4. **ADC channels** – Analog inputs – voltage measurement and analog sensors (e.g. 0–10 V).
5. **Ethernet port (RJ45)** – LAN connection.
6. **Universal inputs and expansion interfaces** – I2C, 1-Wire and other bus connectors.
7. **USB-C connector** – Programming, configuration and service.
8. **Configuration button** – Service button for configuration and reset.
9. **OLED display** – Status display, visible through the housing.
10. **LED indicators** – Input, output and operation status LEDs – visible through the housing.

## Safe use

Before installation make sure the wires are not live. Installation should only be carried out by a qualified person in accordance with applicable standards. Do not use the controller without a housing or with a damaged housing. Do not exceed the operating parameters – especially the relay load. Some platforms require an external server, cloud or additional software.
