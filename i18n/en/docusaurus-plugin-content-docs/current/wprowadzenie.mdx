Here you will find step-by-step guides, configuration instructions and information about SMARTBOB controllers and automation systems.

SMARTBOB is a series of universal home automation controllers designed for mounting in a distribution board and for star-topology installations, ready to integrate with various smart home platforms.

## Controller functions
- AC lighting control
- LED lighting control with dimming
- control of roller shutters, blinds and louvres
- control of air conditioning, heating and boilers
- opening gates, wickets and doors
- garden irrigation and water valve control
- reading alarm states from motion, fire and opening sensors
- reading 1-Wire and I2C sensors, e.g. temperature
- reading data from RS485 devices, e.g. an energy meter
- integrating other systems via various protocols

Here you will find all the information you need to configure, start up and expand your installation.

## Controller models
- **PRO – two levels in a 12 DIN housing**
    - `SM-PRO-3236D`
- **LITE – 12 DIN housing**
    - `SM-LITE-1616R`
    - `SM-LITE-1624D`
- **MINI – 7 DIN housing**
    - `SM-MINI-0808R`
- **GATE – 4 DIN housing**
    - `SM-GATE-0202R (with or without 868 MHz radio)`
:::tip
R – relay outputs, D – digital outputs; the numbers, e.g. 1616R, mean 16 inputs and 16 relay outputs.
:::

SMARTBOB controllers are based on an open architecture (ESP32 processor) and can work with various platforms.

## Supported platforms
* `SUPLA`
* Home Assistant via `ESPHOME`
* `Loxone`
* `Ampio`
* Other systems based on `ESPHOME` or the `MQTT` protocol
* You can also upload your own software or other open distributions

As standard, our devices are delivered with ready-to-use software and a basic configuration that you can easily adapt to your needs.

SMARTBOB solutions are built for flexibility, openness and the ability to grow the system together with the user.
