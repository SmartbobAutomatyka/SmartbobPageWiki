## The SUPLA platform

SUPLA is an open-source POLISH smart home platform for controlling, monitoring and automating devices in a building over the local network and the Internet. It brings building automation, energy, HVAC and security devices together in one ecosystem.

SMARTBOB works with SUPLA as an independent actuator controller and input/output system, making it possible to build extensive building automation installations based on open standards.

---

## How the SUPLA platform works
SUPLA itself does not make end devices. The platform mainly provides:
- software and server infrastructure,
- mobile apps,
- documentation and communication protocols.

:::info
SUPLA is always free for every user!
Anyone can add both official devices and devices based on open code and DIY projects to their account at no cost.
The platform is funded by fees paid by authorised device manufacturers such as SMARTBOB.
By buying a SMARTBOB product you contribute to the development of the platform.
:::

:::warning
On marketplaces such as ALLEGRO you can find devices of other brands, e.g. SONOFF, reprogrammed for SUPLA.
Keep in mind that this voids the device warranty and the device has not passed the appropriate tests. Despite the seller's good intentions, various problems may occur.
:::

## Compatible devices
Devices working with SUPLA fall into two main categories.

### Certified partners and manufacturers
The first group are devices supplied by authorised partners and manufacturers cooperating with SUPLA:
- officially supported,
- tested for compatibility,
- ready to work with the SUPLA app,
- regularly updated.

The list of manufacturers keeps growing; the current device catalogue is here:
- [SUPLA CATALOGUE](https://www.supla.org/pl/devices)

The best-known SUPLA device manufacturers today are:
- [ZAMEL](https://supla.zamel.com/)
- [AURATON](https://auraton.pl/)
- [HEATPOL](https://heatpol.pl/)

### Devices based on open source code

The second group are devices built or modified by the community and integrators.

Most often these are:
- own ESP32 / ESP8266 projects,
- devices of other manufacturers reprogrammed for SUPLA,
- devices based on open firmware,
- individual integrator solutions.

Some of them use universal firmware projects such as:
[GUI Generic SUPLA](https://gui-generic-builder.supla.io/guide/kompilacja/builder-online)
- the SUPLA library for ARDUINO,
- own firmware implementations,
- community projects.

## Where SMARTBOB is with SUPLA today (as of 06.2025)
- SMARTBOB has dedicated software for every controller, so the user does not need to know the details of ESP32 configuration.
- We have signed a partner agreement with the SUPLA platform and actively support the development of its ecosystem, also through cooperation and partner fees.
- We are currently producing and authorising the first SMARTBOB controller, SM-MINI-0808R, so the brand is not yet in the official SUPLA device catalogue.
